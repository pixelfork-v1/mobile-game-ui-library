# Icon sheet prompts — Neon Cyber, extension set

Sci-fi extras, the two coloured navigation sets (required: tabs never show white glyphs), and shop art.
Heading format: `## Sheet ID — title (COLSxROWS, WIDTHxHEIGHT, CANVAS)`; defaults 3x3, 1024x1024, 160.

## Sheet N1 — Navigation, coloured

`home-color shop-color settings-color map-color mail-color calendar-color profile-color friends-color quest-color`

```
A 3x3 grid sprite sheet of 9 separate coloured navigation icons for a neon sci-fi mobile game.

ONE SINGLE IMAGE ONLY, on a fully TRANSPARENT background (alpha channel) — no backdrop, no tiles, no cards, no panels, no frames, no shadow on the ground, nothing behind the objects at all. Do not produce variations, alternatives or options, and do not show the same icons twice in different styles. One square image, one version.

Style: glossy neon sci-fi game item icons, like a cyberpunk or space arcade game. Smooth semi-3D shapes in dark metal and glass, lit by bright neon colour — cyan, magenta, electric purple, acid green — with glowing emissive edges and small light accents. Each object has a thin outline in a darker shade of its own colour. Front view with a slight tilt, chunky and readable, filling its cell. The glow stays close to each object and fades to fully transparent well before the next one. Original designs only — nothing that copies a character or item from an existing game. Not pixel art, not realistic photo, not flat vector.

All objects share one lighting direction, one level of detail and one optical size — a set from the same game, drawn by the same artist in the same sitting. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no hands, no background scenery. Arrange them as a 3 by 3 grid, evenly spaced, each centred in its own ninth with clear empty space around it.

Row 1: a small futuristic base dome with a cyan light, a neon shop kiosk with a magenta sign, a chrome gear with a glowing cyan core.
Row 2: a holographic map with a pink marker, a sleek envelope with a glowing cyan seal, a digital calendar panel with one day lit in magenta.
Row 3: a round helmeted pilot portrait with a cyan visor, two helmeted pilot portraits side by side, a glowing data chip with a gold star.
```
